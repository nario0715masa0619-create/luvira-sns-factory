## One-Line Summary

H007「若作りしないジャケット」を実用情報型に構造化した OPS-008 は、strict 24h 計測を失い、約24h42分時点で 22 impressions / 0 engagement に留まった。information_structure 仮説は **inconclusive** のままである。

## What We Learned

1. **Measurement discipline is the current bottleneck.** 4 of the last 5 runs (OPS-005〜008) lack strict 24h metrics. No structure or angle hypothesis can be reliably evaluated until this is fixed.
2. **Practical information alone did not create an immediate distribution spike** in this run, but a single imperfect observation is not grounds for rejection.
3. **Posting at midnight may reduce early impressions.** This is a plausible confounder, not a proven cause.

## What We Did NOT Learn

- Whether `information_structure=practical_information` is better or worse than `empathy/awareness` for H007.
- Whether H007 as a content_angle has repeatable distribution potential.
- Whether the 40代男性ファッション audience prefers fashion-fit advice over other angles.

## Recommended Follow-Up

- Select OPS-009 from a **different content_angle** than H007 to diversify exploration.
- Make strict 24h measurement a hard gate for any future result_label other than `non_comparable`.
- Review the Autonomous Hypothesis Cycle pilot artifact for the full OPS-009 recommendation and process improvements.
