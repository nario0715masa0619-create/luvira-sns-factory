# Step 01 Pattern Miner Output

## Source Post Structure Pattern

1. **Hook**: Personal realization frame — "40代になって、〜が意外と大事だと気づいた"
2. **Theme statement**: Cleanliness / self-care is revealed in small daily habits, not expensive items.
3. **Three concrete examples**: Nails / hair / scent — each a small, actionable habit.
4. **Value shift**: "High-end products aren't necessary; small habits change impressions."
5. **CTA**: Empathy / experience-sharing question (not "what's essential?" format).

## Emotion Drivers

- **Empathy**: Readers recognize their own daily routines.
- **Relief**: No need for expensive products.
- **Discovery**: Small habits matter more than assumed.
- **Self-efficacy**: "I can do this today."

## Reusable Framework

```text
[Personal realization hook]
→ [Theme: small daily habits > expensive items]
→ [3 concrete examples]
→ [Value shift]
→ [Empathy CTA]
```

## Product-specific Notes

- For 40s men / business owners / sales professionals.
- Avoid preachy tone; use peer-to-peer voice.
- Visual change themes (nails, hair, scent) benefit strongly from images.

## Risk Notes

- Avoid "must", "absolute", "youth", "attractiveness" claims.
- Avoid comparison hooks that worked poorly in OPS-002/004.
- Avoid "みんなの〜何が必須？" CTA format.
