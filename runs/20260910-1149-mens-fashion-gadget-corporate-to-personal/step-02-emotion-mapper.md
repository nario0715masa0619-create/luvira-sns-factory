# Step 02 Emotion Mapper Output

## Primary Emotion

- **Empathy** — "I notice the same small things"

## Secondary Emotions

- **Relief** — no expensive products required
- **Discovery** — small habits have outsized impact
- **Self-efficacy** — easy to start today

## Engagement Driver

Readers feel that "being put-together" is achievable through tiny, low-cost habits rather than luxury grooming.

## Reaction Design

- Encourage replies like "I do the same" or "I started X and noticed Y"
- Avoid broad questions; use specific, low-friction prompts.

## Notes

- Nails / hair / scent are visually concrete → image recommendation stays strong.
- Corporate-to-personal: remove any corporate "should" tone; keep it observational.
