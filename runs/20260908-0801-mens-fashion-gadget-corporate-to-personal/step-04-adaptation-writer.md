## Adaptation Notes

### Source → Target Mapping

| Source Element | Target Adaptation |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Corporate/media hook "40代男性の靴の手入れ" | Personal hook: "40代になって気づいたこと" |
| "週1回の手入れをすべきです" framing | "週1回やるだけで変わる" with first-person voice |
| List of care steps as advice | List of care steps as "自分がやってる" examples |
| "保存/フォロー" CTA | Low-cost empathy question CTA |
| High-level value statement | Peer-to-peer insight: "高い靴じゃなくて、手入れが行き届いてるか" |

### Tone Adjustments

- **Before (corporate)**: "40代男性は、靴の手入れを週1回行うべきです。"
- **After (personal)**: "40代、靴の手入れを週1回にしたら、見た目が変わった気がする。"

- **Before**: "以下の3つの手入れを実践してください。"
- **After**: "自分がやってるのは3つ。"

- **Before**: "保存しておくと役立ちます。"
- **After**: "みんなもやってる？"

### Persona

- Use first-person or subject-drop.
- Allowed: 僕 / 私 / 自分 / 主語省略
- Avoid corporate 「〜です」「〜ます」 endings.
- Avoid 「〜すべきだ」「〜しなければならない」.

### CTA Adaptation

- From source: save / follow / bookmark
- To target: reply / discussion / experience_sharing
- Examples:
  - "靴磨き、ちゃんとやってる人どれくらいいる？"
  - "同じ人いる？"
  - "雨の日の手入れ、やってる？"
  - "週1回やってる？"

### Risk Controls

- Do not claim "everyone should do this."
- Avoid specific brand names.
- Do not invent past purchase stories.
- Keep claims as personal observation or feeling.
- Avoid lecturing tone.

## Candidate Directions

1. **Rain day focus**: dry wipe after rain, care after wet shoes
2. **Business meeting focus**: quick polish before important meetings
3. **Weekend routine focus**: 10-minute Sunday shoe care
4. **Comparison focus**: expensive shoes vs. well-maintained shoes
5. **Personal story focus**: what changed after starting shoe care
