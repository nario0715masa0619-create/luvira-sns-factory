## Adaptation Notes

### Source → Target Mapping

| Source Element | Target Adaptation |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Corporate/media hook "身の回りを整えると印象が変わる" | Personal hook: "自分が気づいた小さなこと" |
| "服を買う前に" framing | "服を買い足す前に" with first-person voice |
| List of items as advice | List of items as "自分が整えた/気をつけた" examples |
| "保存/フォロー" CTA | Question-based reply/discussion CTA |
| High-level value statement | Peer-to-peer insight: "高級品じゃなくて、整ってるかどうか" |

### Tone Adjustments

- **Before (corporate)**: "40代男性は、服を買い足す前に身の回りの小物を整えるべきです。"
- **After (personal)**: "40代、服を買い足す前に、身の回りの小物を整えたら意外と変わった。"

- **Before**: "以下の3つを実践してください。"
- **After**: "自分が気をつけてるのは3つ。"

- **Before**: "保存しておくと役立ちます。"
- **After**: "みんなも何かある？"

### Persona

- Use first-person or subject-drop.
- Allowed: 僕 / 私 / 自分 / 主語省略
- Avoid corporate 「〜です」「〜ます」 endings.

### CTA Adaptation

- From source: save / follow / bookmark
- To target: reply / discussion / experience_sharing
- Examples:
  - "みんなの必須小物、何？"
  - "40代、身だしなみどこから整えた？"
  - "意外と効く小物、教えてほしい"

### Risk Controls

- Do not claim "everyone should do this."
- Avoid specific brand names.
- Do not invent past purchase stories.
- Keep claims as personal observation or feeling.

## Candidate Directions

1. **Gadget focus**: cables, earphones, pouch, phone case
2. **Grooming focus**: shoes, nails, hair, scent
3. **Wallet/bag focus**: slim wallet, gadget pouch, key case
4. **Combined small habits**: mix of gadget + grooming items
5. **Before/after realization**: what changed after tidying small things
